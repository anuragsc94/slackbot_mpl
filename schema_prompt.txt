MPL Battle, Currency, and Revenue Model — Column-Linked Summary
1. Core identifiers & context

Battle / Gameplay unit

Each record corresponds to a single battle or gameplay event, uniquely identified by battle_id (unique ID of each battle/gameplay).

The date on which the game/battle was played is stored in dt.

The exact timestamp of the battle is stored in ist_time.

User identity

The player participating in the battle is identified by user_id (unique user identity).

Game & platform metadata

The specific game played is identified using game_id.

The app/platform context is captured via app_type (Cash/IOS/Android) and app_version.

The user’s country is identified by country_code.

Battle classification

transaction_type distinguishes gameplay/battle events (e.g. “Gameplay-Battles”) from non-gameplay events.

battle_state indicates the lifecycle status of the battle (e.g. CLOSED).

2. Lobby types and how battles are played

Lobby currency type

lobby_type defines the currency system used in the battle:

CASH → cash lobbies

GOLD → gold lobbies

Free (ZSH) lobbies

is_zsh_lobby = "true" indicates a free lobby, where:No entry fee is required.

If the user wins, they receive winning cash.

HOF battles
is_hof_battle indicates whether the battle belongs to a Hall-of-Fame (HOF) lobby.

Terminology mapping:
- "HOF lobby", "HOF battle", "Hall of Fame" => use column is_hof_battle (STRING true/false)
- "Free lobby", "ZSH lobby", "zero entry lobby" => use column is_zsh_lobby (STRING true/false)


Rank-based mechanics are captured via:

rank, score

rank1_target_score, rank2_target_score, rank3_target_score

rank1_multiplier, rank2_multiplier, rank3_multiplier

3. Entry fee and amount collected

Entry into a battle

The total entry fee required to play a battle is stored in entry_fee.

Gross amount collected

gmv represents the total amount collected from the battle, before payouts and expenses.

4. Wallet currencies (inputs: money used to play)

MPL supports multiple wallet currencies, which can be partially combined in cash lobbies.

Bonus Cash

Promotional wallet credit given by the platform.

Amount of bonus cash used to play a battle is stored in in_bc.

Winning Cash (used as input)

Winning cash from previous battles reused as entry is stored in in_wc.

Deposit Cash

Non-withdrawable, real-like cash credited by MPL; can only be used to play.

Usage is tracked via in_dc.

Ticket / other credits

Special ticket-based credits used for entry are stored in in_ticket.

Gold currency

Amount of gold used to play a gold lobby battle is stored in in_gold.

5. Winnings and payouts (outputs: money earned)

Overall winnings

winnings represents the total amount distributed to the user if they won the battle.

Winning Cash

Real, withdrawable cash credited after winning is stored in out_wc.

This includes winnings from cash lobbies and free (ZSH) lobbies.

Bonus Cash earned

Bonus cash credited after winning is stored in out_bc.

Deposit Cash earned

Deposit (non-withdrawable) cash credited after winning is stored in out_dc.

Gold winnings

In gold lobbies, winnings are paid only in gold and tracked via out_gold.

Ticket payouts

Ticket-based winnings are stored in out_ticket.

Outcome indicator

is_won indicates whether the user won or lost the battle.

6. Bonus cash utilisation

Bonus cash burn

bc_utilised captures the net bonus cash consumed in a battle:

BC utilised = Bonus Cash Used − Bonus Cash Earned

7. Platform economics & margins

Platform rake

rake_rate represents the platform rake percentage (where applicable).

Platform margin

mpl_margin is the theoretical margin earned by the platform from the battle.

Expenses

te → Tournament expense (burn for cross-lobby battles).

iae → In-app expenses incurred by the platform.

Gross Margin

gm is computed as:

gm = mpl_margin − te − iae − bc_utilised

Tax

tax is the tax incurred by the platform on MPL margin.

Contribution Margin

cm1 is defined as:

cm1 = gm − tax

8. Skill and system flags

Player skill

skill_score represents the skill score of the user.
